# Human observations

The following captures the user's assessment after directly chatting with the model and asking it to build a Space Invaders-style game.

## Chat

> 反応速度ははやいけど、会話の内容はかなり初期のGPT（3.5とか？）と話しているような感じ。言葉遣いや内容、会話の深み、自然さなど。これをわざわざ使う気は起きないというのが正直なところ。

Interpretive tags:

- Responsiveness: Fast
- Language and tone: Noticeably less natural than current hosted assistants
- Depth: Shallow
- Voluntary adoption: Would not choose to use it
- GPT-3.5 comparison: A subjective analogy, not a benchmark-equivalence claim

## Coding

> これも初期のコーディングエージェントに近い。最初は操作もできなかった。例えばヒット判定やスコアなどもないし、敵の動きにも特に工夫がない。コードをかけて細かく指示が出せる人だったらまたちがうかもしれないけど、僕レベルでは実用に耐えられない。やり取りも英語になった。

Observed shortcomings:

- The first result was not controllable.
- Core game behavior such as collision detection was absent.
- Scoring was absent.
- Enemy movement lacked meaningful design or variation.
- The user would have needed coding knowledge and detailed steering to obtain a better result.
- The interaction drifted into English despite the user's Japanese context.

Human-experience summary:

- Autonomy: Low
- Supervision required: High
- Product judgment: Weak
- Accessibility to a non-expert coder: Insufficient
- Practical daily use: No
- Overall feel: Similar to an early coding agent
